The premise

Faith is the only medium that kept humans obeying one group for eons. But when faith becomes irrational, humans question the system.

Five priests are murdered. They were the only safety net against catastrophe, because they alone knew a sacred ritual. Now every finger and every gun points at one man.

The author

Author. Nothing else.

Mohit Achra is an indie author, visual artist and entrepreneur from India. His first novel, Soul of the Demon God, a stand-alone fantasy, reached readers worldwide in November 2019. In 2021 he followed it with Revenge of the Lost Family, a detective thriller about a city, a fugitive and a secret buried for eight years.

Beyond books, he founded Artembryo, a brand that backs young artists across the country. With Stars are Ugly, he turns to faith, power and the price of questioning both.
